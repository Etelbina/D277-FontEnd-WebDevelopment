About This Project

Discover Washington is an educational website designed to introduce visitors to the state of Washington through three featured cities: Olympia, Seattle, and Bellevue. Each city page presents historical background, demographic information, regional context, notable attractions, and interesting facts using a consistent and accessible layout. The website also includes a contact page featuring a form with client-side validation implemented in JavaScript.

Sources and Citations

The historical, demographic, and geographic information presented throughout this website was compiled from official government websites and local historical organizations. Population and economic statistics were obtained primarily from the U.S. Census Bureau, while state and city information was referenced from official government websites. Historical information was supplemented with resources provided by local historical societies where appropriate.
